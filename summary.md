## 2. Scenario A: Rating Traps Deep Forensic Analysis

*Definition: Deceptively High Display Rating (Stars $\ge 4.0$) masking severe negative feedback (Negative Sentiment $\ge 35\%$).*

### 2.1 The Two Anomaly Sub-Cohorts
1. **Severe Traps ($\ge 50\%$ Negative Share):** **162 Products** (Extreme risk of consumer deception).
2. **Moderate Traps ($35\% - 49.9\%$ Negative Share):** **278 Products** (Emerging defect decay).

### 2.2 Root-Cause Defect Taxonomy (497 Verified Negative Reviews Audited)
To determine whether dissatisfaction was caused by carrier shipping or inherent product defects, reviews were classified into root-cause buckets:

| Defect Category | Complaint Count | Share (%) | Forensic Indicator / Customer Quotes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Product Defect / Quality** | **243** | **48.89%** | Motor burnt out, cracked housing, snapped gear |
| **Size & Fitment Flaws** | **117** | **23.54%** | Roller loose, hole spacing mismatched, wrong diameter |
| **General Quality Dissatisfaction** | **103** | **20.72%** | Thin plastic, cheap material vs. original OEM |
| **Delivery & Logistics Failures** | **26** | **5.23%** | Late delivery, crushed transit package |
| **Packaging Failures** | **8** | **1.61%** | Missing secondary seal, torn polythene |

$$\text{Combined Manufacturing \& Specification Flaws} = 48.89\% + 23.54\% = \mathbf{72.43\%}$$
*Conclusion: Only 5.23% is carrier-related; over 72% represents manufacturing or fitment design flaws.*

### 2.3 Buyer Verification Audit (Anti-Sabotage Verification)
* **Verified Customer Purchases:** **694 reviews** (67.3% density).
* **Takeaway:** Complaints are not generated by competitor review bombing; they stem from authentic paying customers who installed the appliance part.

### 2.4 Temporal Quality Decay (2015 – 2023)
Historical tracking revealed that high star ratings are an artifact of historical volume masking recent quality decay:

| Cohort Year | Negative Complaint Share (%) | Operational Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2015 – 2017** | ~63.0% | Moderate steady failure rate |
| **2018** | **55.7%** | Lowest recorded failure rate |
| **2019** | 63.5% | Initial post-pandemic degradation |
| **2020** | 69.5% | Supply-chain material substitution phase |
| **2022** | **75.8% (All-time Peak)** | Critical manufacturing breakdown |

### 2.5 Failure Timeframe & Appliance Domain Breakdown
* **Failure Timeframe Post-Purchase:**
  * **Unspecified Timeframe:** 76.9%
  * **Immediate / Dead on Arrival (< 1 Week):** **8.9%**
  * **Over 6 Months / 1 Year (Wear Fatigue):** **7.0%**
  * **1 – 6 Months (Medium Term):** **5.6%**

### 2.6 Social Proof & Helpful-Vote Impact
* Single critical reviews often dominate purchase decisions: Product `B07JJQR94P` (⭐ 4.0) received a single 1-star warning with **74 Helpful Upvotes**, multiplying its negative impact.
* **Enforcement Executed:** Automated Vendor Action Notices (`VENDOR_NOTICE_[ASIN].txt`) generated for top offending SKUs demanding a 14-day Corrective Action Plan.

## 3. Scenario B: Hidden Gems & Underrated Products Analysis

*Definition: Display Star Rating is Artificially Low (Stars $\le 3.0$) despite overwhelming customer praise (Positive Sentiment $\ge 65\%$).*

### 3.1 Diagnostic Audit Results (174 Products Evaluated)
* **Average Display Rating:** ⭐ **2.98 / 5.00**
* **Average Positive Review Share:** 🟢 **88.4%**

| Forensic Verdict | Product Count | Share (%) | Mean Reviews | Root Cause Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sample Size Truncation** | **172** | **98.85%** | **1.2** | Too few reviews (1–3 total). One 1-star review mathematically tanks the score. |
| **Suspected Review Bombing** | **1** | **0.57%** | **3.0** | Unverified account (`Verified=False`) gave 1-star, destroying verified 5-stars. |
| **Polarized Opinions** | **1** | **0.57%** | **4.0** | Extreme split (1-star vs 5-stars, rating variance $\ge 2.0$). |

### 3.2 Case Study Drill-Down
1. **Product `B0BDFT8DZR` (Nugget Ice Maker):** Display Score: **3.00★** | Positive: **75.0%**  
   * Praise: *"The 'good' ice... mostly happy with this one."*  
   * Rating Killer: One buyer gave 1-star citing *"smaller ice bucket than expected"*, dragging the mean to 3.0.
2. **Product `B000OGS2OG` (Front/Top Loader Washer):** Display Score: **3.00★** | Positive: **66.7%**  
   * Rating Killer: Unverified account left 1-star review (*"Worst product I've EVER purchased"*), destroying 5-star verified purchase ratings.

### 3.3 Bayesian Recovery Math & Opportunity Roadmap
Using the recovery formula:
$$X = \left\lceil \frac{4.0 \cdot N - \sum R_i}{5.0 - 4.0} \right\rceil$$

* **Recovery Feasibility:**
  * **EASY (< 3 Reviews Needed):** **168 Products (96.5%)**
  * **MEDIUM (3 – 5 Reviews Needed):** **5 Products (2.9%)**
  * **HARD (> 5 Reviews Needed):** **1 Product (0.6%)**
* **Top Recoverable Items:** `B01DKC2GQ0`, `B00Q9EJ45S`, `B0C8TR1SWY` need **only 2 additional 5-star reviews**; `B095RGB1WT`, `B09PL5HHQJ` need **only 1 review** to surpass 4.0 Stars.
* **Strategic Business Value:** No product redesign or manufacturing fix required. Merchandising teams can deploy automated review-request flows to restore 174 quality items to prominent catalog visibility.

## 6. DuckDB Gold Layer Data Schema Reference

All processed tables generated during this analysis are stored in `warehouse.duckdb`:

1. `gold_product_discrepancy_flags`: Base classification of all catalog products into Scenario A, B, C, or Nominal.
2. `gold_scenario_a_root_causes`: Keyword and topic breakdown of defect types across Scenario A items.
3. `gold_scenario_a_impact_ranking`: Products sorted by weighted dissatisfaction score:
   $$\text{Score} = (1 + \text{helpful\_votes}) \cdot \text{neg\_reviews}$$
4. `gold_scenario_a_durability`: Failure timeframe and appliance part classification.
5. `gold_scenario_b_forensics`: Classification of underrated items (Sample Truncation vs. Review Bombing vs. Polarized).
6. `gold_scenario_b_recovery_plan`: Exact calculations of additional reviews needed to reach $\ge 4.0$ stars.
7. `gold_scenario_c_catalog_purge`: Target list of confirmed lemons recommended for catalog de-listing.
